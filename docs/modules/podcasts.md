# Podcasts (local files, P0)

Round 3 (search to add a show, behind a Settings switch) is the last section, "Search". Code: `patch/pcmodel.c` (shows, episodes, the metadata merge, the resume ring, Continue Listening's `pc_continue`, speeds, `pc_note`), `patch/podcasts.c` (the page), `patch/podcasts.h`. Asset: `tools/pod_ui.py`
`build_podcasts_page` (`ui/m1plus_podcasts.bin`). Tests: `test/pod/podcasts.py` (plus the Daily Mix case in `test/pod/dailymix.py`), `test/pod/pcshows.py` (the Shows page), `test/pod/pcshow.py` (the Show page). One hook (the tag reader's call site, "Tags"); the network side (the refresh, Downloads) is a worker thread with no hook.

## What it is

Files the user copies to the card: `/mnt/mmc/Podcasts/<Show>/<episode files>` (the folder name is matched case-insensitively: `pc_root` gives the spelling the card has),
audio extensions `mp3 m4a m4b aac ogg opus flac wav wma`, an optional `cover.jpg|png|jpeg` or `folder.*` per show. The Extras item **Podcasts** (item id 20, saved
character `l`, Library group (last), hidden from the menu by default, `ACT_PODCASTS`) opens one window, `m1plus_podcasts`, with three levels switched in place (Back goes up a level first):

* **Shows**: the Apple Podcasts style page, see "The Shows page" below: the large title, the refresh line, Continue Listening, the Downloads row and the grid of every show. A cover is decoded one a tick (16 ms) only after
  `pc_cover_ok` read the picture's header (JPEG SOF / PNG IHDR) and found at most 2.1 million pixels: the stock loader holds the whole bitmap, so a 3000 px cover is refused (placeholder disc).
  No Podcasts folder or no show: a hint (`label_status`) that says how to start.
* **Downloads**: the queue, the failed and the paused downloads (see "Downloads"), behind the Shows level's Downloads row.
* **Show**: the Apple Podcasts style page, see "The Show page" below: a tinted header in the album page's manner (160 px cover, name, one Latest Episode pill, the show's description, "Category · Author"), a fixed height cell per episode
  (every episode the show knows, newest first, on the card or not), the navbar's "... | Now Playing" capsule whose "..." opens the show's bottom sheet.

A played episode has a grey title and a check in its date line; one on the card a light disc there; the pill says what a tap does (play, download, pause, resume, retry). There is a way to mark an episode played by hand again: the cell's "..." sheet (Mark as Played / Unplayed) and the show's (Mark All as Played).
Cells are painted by the content view (no widget per cell), the tile ring is 44, the cell layouts 8.

## Files (the formats the next phase writes)

Per show folder, both optional, UTF-8, one record per line, `\n` or `\r\n`:

```
.episodes   M1PLUS-EPISODES 1
            g=<guid>\tp=<pubDate, seconds since 1970>\td=<duration s>\tl=<enclosure length bytes>\tf=<local file name>\tt=<title>\tu=<enclosure url>\tx=<description>
.show       M1PLUS-SHOW 1
            title=<name>
            feed=<feed url>
            image=<image url>
            checked=<seconds since 1970 of the last refresh>
            author=<the channel's itunes:author>
            category=<the first itunes:category>
            desc=<the channel's summary, plain text>
```

`.episodes`: tab separated `key=value` fields, any order, unknown keys ignored, `#` lines ignored, an entry needs one of `g f t u`, values have no tab or newline (the writer
strips them). `u` is the **enclosure url as the feed gives it** (never the redirected, signed one: it expires); `f` is the file name in the show folder (while a download runs
`f` + `.part` exists); `x` is the episode's **description as plain text** (optional; see "Parser"). The model keeps guid (as an FNV-1a hash), pub, dur, length, file, title and `x` (into `pc_ep_t.desc`, `PC_DESC` 208 bytes: at most
`PC_DESC_CUT` 200 bytes of content and the NUL, cut before a UTF-8 continuation byte) and ignores `u` (the downloader reads it); a title is cut to 95 bytes, a file name over 127 bytes is no name. At most 64 KB of the file is read and 300 episodes kept (the newest).
`.show`: `key=value` lines (keys case-insensitive, unknown ones ignored, an absent key leaves the field empty): `title` (95 bytes), `feed` and `image` (`PC_URL` 320), `checked`, and the descriptive keys written **only when not empty**: `author=` (`pc_show_t.author`, `PC_AUTHOR` 64: at most 63 bytes),
`category=` (`category`, `PC_CAT` 48: at most 47 bytes), `desc=` (`desc`, `PC_DESC` 208, written with at most 200 bytes). A value has no tab or newline and is cut on a character boundary. Struct sizes: `pc_ep_t` 456 bytes (was 248; `desc` at 248), `pc_show_t` 1332 (was 1008; `author` at 1008, `category` 1072, `newest` at 1328: the Shows page's order, see "The Shows page",
`desc` 1120); 300 episodes are 137 kB (was 74 kB), and `pc_load_show`'s transient array of episodes and files 319 kB.

Without `.episodes`: title = file name without its extension, date = the file's modification time, duration unknown. With it, a file with an entry takes the entry's title, date and
duration. The one place they meet is `pm_merge` (`pcmodel.c`): entry + file = `PC_LOCAL`; entry with only `name.part` = `PC_PART`; entry with neither = `PC_REMOTE`; file with no entry = new
`PC_LOCAL`. The Show page lists all three states (every show is on the Shows level, with a file on the card or not; a show with metadata and no episode says "No downloaded episodes" or "No episodes yet..." under its header). Sort: date descending, then file name descending (a numbered series reads
from its last), then entry order.

State on the card (written only while the card is mounted, `pod_card_mounted`, atomically: temp file, fsync, rename; `/mnt/mmc/.m1plus/podcasts/`):

* `resume`: `"PCR1"`, slot count (word), then 16-byte slots `hash, pos, len, flags` (flags 1 = played), most recent first, at most 512 (q2-pod's ring size). The hash is FNV-1a over the
  ASCII-lower-cased full path. A bad magic, a count over 512 or a short file is an empty ring.
* `speed`: `"PCS1"`, count, 8-byte pairs `show hash (of the folder name), mode`, at most 64 (oldest out).

## Resume, played, speed

`pc_note(track)` is called from `recent_note` (so from Now Playing's tick, the mini bar's tick and, dark or lit, the page's own 1 s timer) with the playing record (`cc_playing`). Nothing else
runs by itself: **a place is saved only while one of those pages is in front** (positions lag, and an episode that ends while another page is in front is only marked played if the
last look was within its last 30 s or 95 %). For a path under `/mnt/mmc/podcasts/` (any case):

* it reads `get_playtimeandlength`; a place of 30 s or more is stored (`pm_set`); within 30 s of the start nothing is stored (a saved place stays); within 30 s of the end, or past 95 %, the
  episode is played (place cleared); heard again from the middle it is a place again. The ring is written at most every 30 s, at once when paused, at a track change, on the page's actions and when the page goes to the background.
* a saved place of 30 s or more: once the episode plays (status 2) with 1..4 s elapsed, `seek_time(place)` (the stock key-path seek) jumps there; nothing is recorded meanwhile or for 2 s after; a jump not done after 15 s is dropped. A played episode starts at 0 (the page tap calls `pc_restart`).
* speed: when an episode starts its show's mode goes into `playspeed_mode` and `mclSetPlaySpeed(mode)` (the page also does this before starting). When music plays again (and 15 s
  have passed since the page set it) the mode seen before goes back. Modes: 0 1x, 1 1.25x, 2 1.5x, 3 2x (stock "Multiple Speed", images `p_1_25`, `p_1_5`, `p_2`; `config_init` reads a stored value >= 4 as 0). Only RAM: the stock `[PLAYSET] PLAYSPEED` is not touched. The stock player may take a changed
  speed at the next track open (q2-pod sends it before opening for that reason).

## Keeping podcasts out

Daily Mix candidates (`dm_ph_lib`), the Recently Played shelf (`recent_note`) and every Shuffle of ours (`shuffle_play` all three kinds, the Songs page's Shuffle pill, the Pod's Shuffle Songs:
`pc_strip` copies the queue without episodes, only when there are some) skip paths under `/mnt/mmc/Podcasts/`. The stock lists that read `songtable` (Songs, Albums, Artists, Genres, Composers, Hi-Res, Recently Added, search, an album's songs) no longer show scanned episodes (the query filter, payload.md hook 6); Folders, Favourites, Recently Played and Most Played (own tables, lists of copies) still do (those hold the records as played or added; ours skip podcasts for Daily Mix, Recently Played and the Shuffles, which stays needed for them and for the queues). **Why every query, no scope flag** (q2-pod scopes because its Cover Flow and Folders also read `songtable`): here the DB is only read by Library lists, Cover Flow's album source (`getAllAlbum`), Memory Playback's library queue (`getAllMusic`) and the batch Add/Delete on those lists, and a podcast is never in them after the filter; episodes play as class 1 (folder) queues, the Podcasts pages list directories, the scan's duplicate check (`toolsCheckItemExist`) is its own `sqlite3_exec`, and the playlist, `myfav`, recent and often tables are not `songtable`. Queries on other tables, and anything not starting with `select ` and containing ` from songtable` followed by a space, `;` or the end, pass untouched (payload.md hook 6).

## Playing files the library may not have scanned

Stock Folders play builds nothing in the database: `toolsLoadDirectory(dir)` fills a temporary deque (`0xa03964`) of `stSongInfo`, `loadSongList(queue, name, cue, 1)` copies it
(class 1 is not in the `0xf000` class range: the generic path) and `start_player(queue, idx, 1, 2)` / `navigator_to_with_context("playing_page", {queue, idx, 1, 2})` starts it. The page does
the same with records of its own (zeroed `0x58` bytes with name, path, title, album = artist = show title, type 8, class 1, pushed with `_deque_push_back`, which copies the strings), so
playback does not depend on a library scan. `REC_ID` is 0: whether stock then records them in Recently Played / Most Played by path is a device check.

## Addresses and pins

`seek_time` `0x4e0544` (prologue, `move s0,a0`, the CUE add, `jalr mclSetSeek`), `mclSetPlaySpeed` `0x5d281c` (prologue, `move a2,a0` for the `%d` of `{mcl-playspeed\%d}`), data `playspeed_mode`
`0xa05acc`: `PODCAST_PINS` in `tools/pod.py`, checked at build. libc added: `opendir readdir closedir __xstat memmove` (`__xstat(3, path, buf)` is stock's `stat`, struct stat 144 bytes, `st_mtime` +0x40;
`struct dirent` d_name at +11). Directory tests use `opendir` success, never `d_type` or `st_mode`.

## Limits

40 shows, 300 episodes a show (newest), 400 files looked at, names under 128 bytes, `.episodes` 64 KB, 512 places, 64 speeds. Podcasts and the music library share `Podcasts`-named folders case-insensitively only
if the card's file system is case-insensitive; a card spelled `podcasts` is found by listing `/mnt/mmc`.

## Device checks (not run on a device)

1. Does Update Local Music scan `/mnt/mmc/Podcasts` (episodes in Songs, Recently Added) and skip dot files such as `.episodes`? (Dot-folders are skipped by the build: payload.md hook 5. `/mnt/mmc/Podcasts/` is not hidden, so its episodes are probably scanned into `songtable`, but every Library query leaves them out: payload.md hook 6.) Are `.m4b` files accepted by the player (stock's extension list has no `m4b`)?
2. `__xstat` layout: episode dates show the files' dates (a wrong layout shows 1970).
3. A tap on an episode starts it from a record with `REC_ID` 0: Now Playing shows title, show, cover (none), next/previous follow the list; stock Recent / Most Played do not break.
4. Resume: stop an episode at 10 min on Now Playing, play another, tap the first: it jumps to ~10 min within 2 s; reboot keeps places; the card removed: no write, no crash.
5. Speed: set 1.5x on a show, start an episode: plays at 1.5x from the start (or from the next track); music afterwards at the old speed.
6. Large cover (1400 px) decodes without a stall; a 3000 px one shows the disc.
7. Screen off with an episode playing: places still saved (Now Playing / mini bar timers at 1 s).

Tests: `test/pod/pcfeed.py`, `test/pod/pcnet.py`, `test/pod/pcshows.py` (the Shows page); fixtures `test/data/feeds/*-3items.xml` (the three project feeds cut to three items; the whole feeds, optional, go in `work/feeds/{allin,headlines,wsj}.xml`
and the parser test then compares the 20 newest items of each with Python's xml.etree). No new hook, no new import.

* **The Feeds level is gone** (round 2 of the revamp): its Refresh All is the Shows page's refresh line (the guards, the progress dialog and the pausing of downloads are the same code), its Import row is the "..." sheet's Import from Card, and the per-feed words
  (the error in words: `No Wi-Fi`, `Set the date first`, `Certificates missing`, `Certificate problem`, `Can't connect`, `Feed not found`, `Server error`, `Not a podcast feed`, `Storage unavailable`, `Not enough memory`; `N new`) are the grid's captions. Every show, subscribed or local, followed with nothing downloaded or not, is on the Shows page ("The Shows page").
* **Subscribe**: `pn_import` reads `Podcasts/subscriptions.txt` (one http(s) address a line, `#` comments; over-long, spaced or duplicate lines skipped; 40 shows at most), makes `<host>-<hash8>/` with a `.show` (title = the host, `checked=0`);
  the first refresh names the show (title from the feed) and, when the folder holds nothing else (no `.episodes` yet), renames the folder after it. Importing starts a refresh of the shows never refreshed.
* **Worker** (`pn_refresh`, UI thread: the guards, then ONE detached pthread for the whole job, one show at a time): guards = card mounted, `get_wifisignal() > 0` (read on the UI thread only), clock after `PN_MIN_EPOCH` (mid 2025), the CA bundle,
  the firmware's `/etc/m1plus-cacert.pem` (as q2-pod's `/etc/scrobble-ca.pem`, but all of Mozilla's roots as curl extracts them, since a feed and its redirects can be on any host: 118 roots, 200 kB; committed as `assets/cacert.pem`,
  pinned and installed by `tools/mod_cacerts.py`; a name of its own, so stock programs that look in `/etc/ssl` are unchanged; nothing on the card is read). Per show: read `.show`, stream the feed through libcurl into the parser (verification ON, no `SSL_VERIFY*` option ever set; at most 16 redirects (tracking chains reach 8 hops), limited to http(s); `FAILONERROR`; gzip accepted; connect 20 s, 30 s under
  1 kB/s, 120 s total; an 8 MiB cap: a longer feed is parsed up to the cap and kept when a feed and an item were seen by then, since feeds list the newest items first, e.g. TED Talks Daily at 8.5 MB; the item cut by the cap is left out), write `.episodes` then `.show` atomically (temp file, fsync, rename), set the status. The page only **reads** the status record from its 1 s timer (`pn_gen` moved: rows rebuilt from the files), so no widget is touched
  from the thread and `pc_scan_shows` (static sort state) is never called from it.
* **Parser** (`pf_*`): a byte-at-a-time state machine (text / tag / comment / CDATA / declaration) over libcurl's chunks, any split; keeps the channel's title and picture (`itunes:image href`, else `<image><url>`) and the **20 newest items by pubDate**
  (stable; an item needs an enclosure that is not a picture or page): guid (else the url), title, pubDate (RFC 822, zones, 2-digit years), `itunes:duration` (seconds, MM:SS, HH:MM:SS), enclosure url / length / type; entities and CDATA decoded, white
  space collapsed (no tab or newline reaches a file), a 5000 byte title cut at 95 bytes on a character boundary. Atom feeds are "Not a podcast feed". Measured under Unicorn: three whole feeds (4 MB, 441-600 items) in about a second.
  **Descriptions** (`pf_item_t.desc`; the show's `pf_summary`): an item's `<itunes:summary>`, else `<description>`, else `<content:encoded>` (the first one that is not empty after stripping; the channel: `<itunes:summary>`, else `<description>`, not the one inside `<image>`).
  The text arrives entity- and CDATA-decoded, so it holds real HTML and double-encoded entities; it goes byte by byte through a second stage (the description sink: no tree, bounded memory, an element of 100 kB costs nothing after the cut; `pf_dbyte`):
  a tag is skipped (a `<` followed by a letter, `/`, `!` or `?` starts one; quotes inside it are respected so a `>` in an attribute does not end it; `<!-- -->` comments are skipped whole; an unterminated tag or quote swallows the rest;
  any other `<` is text), a boundary tag (`p br li ul ol div tr td h1`-`h6 hr blockquote`, open or close) counts as a space, any other tag as nothing; `&amp; &lt; &gt; &quot; &apos; &nbsp;` and `&#n;` / `&#xh;` (to UTF-8) are decoded once more
  (an unknown, invalid or unterminated one stays as written; a decoded `<` is text, never a tag); white space, control bytes and U+00A0 become one space, the ends are trimmed; then the first 200 bytes are kept, cut before a split UTF-8 character
  (a CJK character is 3 bytes, an emoji 4; no ellipsis in the file). A word that does not fit whole is cut where the byte budget ends (not at a word boundary). An element nested inside a description (XHTML children) is not a boundary: its text is joined. The channel's `itunes:author` (channel level only, not an item's, not `<managingEditor>`;
  at most 63 bytes) and the **first** `<itunes:category text="...">` that is outside an item and has a text (entity-decoded, white space collapsed, at most 47 bytes; a nested sub-category does not replace it) are collected too (`pf_author`, `pf_category`, `pf_summary`).
* **`.episodes` writer**: the kept items first (`g p d l f t u x`; `x=` only when the item has a description), then the **old lines whose file (or `.part`) is on the card and that the feed no longer lists**, copied as written (an `x=` stays). An item with an old line keeps that line's `f`;
  its `x=` is replaced by the description the feed gives now, and **kept when the feed gives none** (an old line's value is cut to 207 bytes);
  a new one is `YYYY-MM-DD Title.ext` (forbidden ExFAT characters → `-`, at most 100 bytes, a clash gets ` (2)`; the extension from the url, else the MIME type, else mp3). `.show` gains `checked=<epoch>` (`pc_show_t.checked`);
  `PC_URL` is now 320 (a feed url longer than that is refused at import).
  `pf_render_show(title, feed, image, checked, author, category, desc, out, cap)` is pure: the refresh (`pn_refresh_one`) passes the feed's author / category / summary, **else the show's old value** (the feed gave an empty one); a caller that has only the show (unsubscribe) passes its fields as they are, and an empty author / category / desc writes no key.
  Tests: `test/pod/pcfeed.py` (the three fixtures and the whole feeds against an `xml.etree` oracle with an independent regular-expression implementation of the strip, decode and cut; HTML, entities, CDATA, preference, UTF-8 cuts, any chunking, the file round trip), `test/pod/pcnet.py` (the refresh keeps old values).
* **Refresh dialog** (`pg_dlg_*` in `patch/podcasts.c`; the widgets are hidden, insensitive children at the end of `ui/m1plus_podcasts.bin`, `tools/pod_ui.py podcasts_dialog`; test `test/pod/pcdialog.py`, preview `tools/preview_podcasts_dialog.py`):
  Refresh All and Import (which refreshes the shows it added and the ones never refreshed) open a modal dialog over the page, built from the Playlists menu's pieces: a full-client scrim (dimmed as that menu's; it takes every touch, so nothing under it and no
  second job can be started) and a 280 x 200 card in its glass (`pl_glass`, patch3_x, as high as that image), centred: the title **Refreshing Podcasts**, the show being fetched (ellipsis in 240 px), a 6 px bar, `2 of 5` (or **Cancelling…**) and a
  **Cancel** row filling the last 50 px (hairline above, pressed 6 % white, white text; the dialog is neutral, no accent colour). The bar is the shows done plus the running show's part, over the number of shows; the worker keeps the part (0..1000) in the status record: the feed's request is nine
  tenths of the show, advancing by bytes when the server gave a length (libcurl's `CURLOPT_XFERINFOFUNCTION`, scaled to 32 bits, no 64-bit division), else in one step; the cover's request is the last tenth. The page reads the record from a page-scoped
  **200 ms timer** (`pg_dlg_tick`) that runs only while the page is in front **and the screen is lit** (dark: it stops itself and the 1 s tick starts it again when the screen is back; the dialog is then still right, or closes at its first tick). No widget
  is touched from the thread. **When the worker has stopped** the dialog closes by itself, the Shows page is read again, and one line is toasted: `3 updated`, `2 updated, 1 failed`, `3 failed` (updated = refreshed, new items or not; the per-feed errors stay in
  the grid's captions), or after Cancel `Cancelled` / `Cancelled, 2 updated`. **Cancel** (and Back, and leaving the page, i.e. the hardware Back key destroying it, which the page treats as Cancel) sets a flag the worker reads between shows and in the
  transfer-info callback: a non-zero return makes libcurl abort the transfer at once (`CURLE_ABORTED_BY_CALLBACK`; `curl_easy_setopt` is all it needs, no new import). The dialog stays, with the row dead and `Cancelling…`, until the thread has really
  ended, so a second refresh cannot start meanwhile. Files stay consistent (every write is atomic); **a show whose transfer was aborted keeps the status it had** (no "Cancelled" on its row; shows not reached keep theirs too); a show whose feed was written
  before Cancel arrived counts as updated, its picture is dropped. Another page in front: the job goes on, the dialog is there when the page comes back. Strings: 8 new keys in all 13 languages (`tools/pod_i18n.py`); the widest title (German) is 231 of 240 px.
* **Cover art** (`patch/pccover.c`, `pv_shrink`; called by `pn_cover` in `patch/pcnet.c`): after a show's `.episodes` and `.show` are written (and the folder renamed), when the folder has **no** cover file (none of the six names the page
  knows; a user's picture always wins, nothing is downloaded) and the picture url (the feed's `image`, else the one already in `.show`) is http(s), the worker downloads it with the feed's own libcurl setup (`pn_easy`: CA bundle, redirects limited
  to http(s), FAILONERROR, the same time-outs; 6 MiB cap, `PN_COVER_CAP`) into `.cover.src`, then `pv_shrink` writes `.cover.tmp` and it is renamed to `cover.jpg` (a half file is never visible; the page picks it up through its 1 s `pn_gen` rebuild:
  the generation moves when the cover lands). **Size**: stock's loader holds the whole bitmap (3000 x 3000 = 36 MB RGBA, the page refuses above 2.1 million pixels) and stock's shrinker `toolsGetExternCover` refuses a JPEG above 1 million pixels, so it is
  not used. `pv_shrink` uses demo's own libjpeg 7 (already imported; the structures by offset, pinned in `tools/pod.py JPEG_PINS` against stock's decoder and encoder): decode at libjpeg's DCT scale 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 or 1/1 (the largest keeping the longer
  side at 192 px or more: 3000 px -> 1/8 = 375 px), one row at a time, an area average to **192 x 192 at most** (aspect kept, never upscaled; `PV_SIZE` = twice the largest cover any Podcasts page draws, the Show header's 96 px, so the grid's area
  average has pixels to average) and a baseline JPEG at quality 85 (about 10 to 20 kB). Memory: libjpeg's row buffers and about 3 kB of sums, whatever the picture's size (the old full decode would be 27 MB or more); time: Huffman decoding only, at 1/8 scale.
  Refused without libjpeg: not JPEG or PNG, a baseline JPEG above 16 million pixels, a progressive one above 2.1 million (libjpeg keeps all its coefficients); CMYK is refused after the header.
  **PNG** (`patch/pcpng.c`, `pv_shrink_png`; All-In's cover is a 2000 x 2000 RGBA PNG): demo imports only `png_read_image` (the whole image) and has no `png_read_row` and no zlib, so the decoder is our own, streaming: it walks the chunks
  (IHDR, PLTE, tRNS, any number of IDAT, CRCs not checked), inflates the IDAT stream (stored, fixed and dynamic blocks; a 32 kB window; input pulled from the file 4 kB at a time, output pushed byte by byte; written afresh in the shape of Mark Adler's
  `puff.c`, zlib licence, credited in the file's header), unfilters one scanline at a time (None, Sub, Up, Average, Paeth; only the previous row is kept) and converts it to RGB (grey, grey + alpha, RGB, RGBA, palette; 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 bit; palette and colour-key tRNS;
  **transparency is composited over white**: a JPEG has no alpha, white is how Apple's own apps show transparent art, and black-on-clear logos stay readable (white-on-clear art would vanish: a rarer case)) before the same area average and encoder. Limits: 8192 px a side and 25 million pixels
  (about 5000 x 5000; Apple's art is 1400 to 3000). **Memory**: one calloc of about 40 kB (window, input buffer, two Huffman tables) plus two scanlines of at most 64 kB (an 8192 px RGBA 16-bit row) and one RGB row of at most 24 kB: under 200 kB
  whatever the picture's size, none of it on the thread's stack (the Huffman table of a dynamic block, 0.6 kB, and the 4 kB output sums apart). **Untrusted data**: every chunk length is checked against the file, every Huffman symbol, length and distance
  against its table and the bytes output so far, every filter byte and palette index, the output against the header's size (a zlib bomb stops at the size the header claims), a truncated or short or long stream fails; any failure leaves no file and
  no status. An **interlaced** (Adam7) PNG cannot be done in one pass over its rows: a large one is refused, one the page can decode as it is (at most 2.1 million pixels) is renamed to `cover.png`. Errors: libjpeg's `error_exit` is stock's own (`0x5dbfe0`: `longjmp` to our `_setjmp`), never its default (`exit`); every file and object is released, `.cover.*` removed. **A cover
  failure changes nothing else**: the feed's status (OK, N new) is its own, nothing is logged, and the next refresh tries again (no marker file: a picture that always fails is downloaded again on every refresh, at most 6 MiB). An existing `cover.jpg` is
  never replaced, so a changed picture upstream is not followed (delete the file to fetch again).

## Downloads (phase 1, step 2: built, not yet run on a device)

Code: `patch/pcnet.c` (`pn_dl_*`, `pn_dmain`, `pn_dl_run`, `pn_dl_attempt`; the queue in the status record), `patch/pcfeed.c` (`pf_find_episode`: the `u` / `l` of an episode's `.episodes` line), `patch/podcasts.c` (the Show level's taps and state lines, the Shows page's Downloads row, `pg_awake_tick`), `patch/curlopts.h`. Tests: `test/pod/pcdl.py` (sections `queue_tests transfer_tests resume_tests failure_tests control_tests refresh_tests page_tests refresh_page_tests awake_tests width_tests`; the scripted libcurl of
`test/pod/harness.py` now serves a `Content-Type`, partial bodies that end in an error, and `on_sleep`). **Credit**: the policy follows q2-podcasts (MIT, github.com/m-d-e-v/q2-podcasts: `internal/library/download.go` and `contrib/shanlingq2/README`); the code is our own, over demo's libcurl.

* **What the user does**: on the Show page **a tap on the pill of an episode that is not on the card queues its download; a tap on a queued or running one pauses it** (its `.part` stays, so a later tap resumes it); the cell's body does nothing for such an episode (only the pill and the "..." download). The pill's states are
  the table in "The Show page" (`↓ 48 MB`, a ring with the percent, `Waiting`, pause glyph + `Paused` (an incomplete `<f>.part` and nothing queued: after a Cancel, a reboot, a standby), `↻ Retry` (a failed one; the reason is on the Downloads level)), and when the file is complete the cell is an ordinary local episode (a play pill and the disc badge; the page reads the show again when the worker's generation moves).
  Toasts: the guards' words on a tap that cannot queue (`No Wi-Fi`, `Set the date first`, `Certificates missing`, `Queue is full`).
  Reasons (the Feeds words plus): `Not found` (HTTP 404 / 410, or no enclosure address in `.episodes`), `Not audio` (the answer is a web page), `Card is full`, `Server error`, `Can't connect`, `Certificate problem`, `Storage unavailable`. Strings: `Downloading Waiting Paused Failed Not found Not audio Card is full Queue is full Downloads`
  (9 keys, 13 languages, `tools/pod_i18n.py`; `test/pod/pcdl.py width_tests` measures every line in the real font: the widest is Spanish at 269 of 274 px).
* **The Shows page's Downloads row**: while anything is queued or running a row **Downloads (3)** shows (the "Downloads row" of "The Shows page"), the plain title style, the one running as its grey line (`Downloading 42%`, else `Waiting`). A tap on it opens the **Downloads level** (below). It appears and goes with the queue.
* **The `.part` file and the files**: `<show folder>/<f>.part`, `f` from `.episodes` (at most 100 bytes: `PF_MAX_FILE`); a path (root, folder, `f`, `.part`) over `PN_DL_PATH_MAX` = 240 bytes is refused (`Storage unavailable`). The data is gathered in a **512 KB buffer** and each chunk is written with `open(O_WRONLY|O_CREAT|O_APPEND)`, `write`, `fsync`, `close`, so the card is
  almost never held busy and a break loses at most what was in the buffer (the bytes got when a transfer ends, in whatever way, are flushed first). At the end the file is `rename`d to `<f>` (the last chunk is fsynced before). **No mtime** is set: demo imports neither `utime` nor `utimes` (the model takes an episode's date from `p=`, not from the file). An empty `.part` (nothing on the card) is removed after a failure or a Cancel; one with bytes stays. A leftover `.part` of an episode whose file is there is removed.
* **Worker**: one detached pthread at a time (`pn.kind` = refresh or download), **never a refresh and downloads together**. `pn_dl_add` (UI thread: the guards of `pn_guard`, the path limit, the queue of `PN_QMAX` = 32, then `pn_dl_start`) fills a slot and starts the thread when none runs; the thread takes the slot that was asked first, one episode at a time, until none is left, then ends. The queue
  is memory only: after a reboot `.part` files show as Paused and a tap resumes them. The page writes a slot and then its state; the thread moves a state by compare-and-swap, the page cancels a waiting slot by compare-and-swap, so neither needs a lock. A start the thread's end raced (an episode queued while the thread was ending) is
  caught by the page's 1 s downloads timer (it calls `pn_dl_start` when slots wait and no thread runs). **`pn_dl_start` runs the guards again**: if they fail then (Wi-Fi gone after a standby, say) every waiting download fails at once with their words, so a dead queue does not keep the device awake.
* **The request** (`pn_easy`, as the feed's): the stored enclosure url `u` (never a final signed redirect), `PN_CA`, redirects (8) limited to http(s), `FAILONERROR`, verification on (no `SSL_VERIFY*` option ever set), no signals, connect 20 s; changed for a download: **low speed 1 byte/s for `PN_DL_STALL` = 60 s** (the stall), **no total time limit**, `Accept-Encoding: identity` (a range of an encoded body would not resume).
  libcurl numbers used (`patch/curlopts.h`, checked against libcurl's `curl/curl.h`, the numbers have not changed since before 7.73): `CURLOPT_RANGE` 10007 (`CURLOPTTYPE_STRINGPOINT + 7`, a `"N-"` string; `CURLOPT_RESUME_FROM_LARGE` 30142 is not used: with a Range option a server that answers 200 is handed over as it is, which is the rule below, whereas
  RESUME_FROM would turn it into an error), `CURLOPT_LOW_SPEED_LIMIT` 19, `CURLOPT_LOW_SPEED_TIME` 20, `CURLOPT_TIMEOUT` 13, `CURLOPT_ACCEPT_ENCODING` 10102, `CURLINFO_RESPONSE_CODE` 0x200002, `CURLINFO_CONTENT_TYPE` 0x100012 (`CURLINFO_STRING + 18`, read in the write callback at the first byte), `CURLE_PARTIAL_FILE` 18, `CURLE_RANGE_ERROR` 33.
* **Resume**: when the `.part` has bytes the request is `Range: N-` (N = its size, `lseek(fd, 0, SEEK_END)`). **A 206 is appended; a 200 starts the file again** (the `.part` is truncated at the first byte; checked in the write callback with `CURLINFO_RESPONSE_CODE`). A 416 or curl's range error (33): the `.part` is dropped and the file starts again, once (a second one fails with `Server error`).
  The `Content-Range` start is not read: a 206 is trusted.
* **Failures**: a **network failure** (any curl error that is not one of the next lines, a stall = `CURLE_OPERATION_TIMEDOUT`, a short body `CURLE_PARTIAL_FILE`) and an HTTP 5xx, 408 or 429 are **retried after 10, 20 and 40 s** (`PN_DL_RETRIES` = 3; the wait is one-second `sleep`s so Cancel and a pause are heard at once; the row says `Waiting`; the count starts again
  after an attempt that got bytes, so a long file over a flaky link is not given up); then `Can't connect` / `Server error`. These **fail at once**: HTTP 404 / 410 (`Not found`), another 4xx (`Server error`), a certificate error (`Certificate problem`; a bad CA file `Certificates missing`), **`Content-Type: text/...`** in any case (`Not audio`: a Wi-Fi sign-in page; an existing `.part` is untouched),
  **a full card**, an empty 200, a `.part` that cannot be opened or written or fsynced, a failed rename (`Storage unavailable`, the finished `.part` stays). **The card keeps 32 MB free** (`PN_DL_MARGIN_KB`): `statfs` before the request (the rest of the file, `l` minus the `.part`, plus 32 MB must fit; an unknown `l` is skipped) and before every chunk (the chunk plus 32 MB); a refusal in the middle keeps the chunks already
  written as the `.part`. A body over 2 GiB (`PN_DL_MAX_BYTES`) is refused. An `l=` that is not a number or does not fit 31 bits is unknown. libcurl decides what is complete (`rc == 0`; it checks `Content-Length`), the length in `.episodes` only sizes the check and the percent (a total smaller than the bytes got was wrong: shown as MB).
* **Refresh versus downloads (the choice): downloads pause for a refresh, then go on.** A tap on Refresh All (or Import) while a download thread runs: the page sets a pause flag (`pn_dl_pause`; the transfer is aborted at once through the transfer-info callback, what was received is flushed, the entry goes back to waiting, the queue is kept), keeps the request
  (`pg.rpend`, the Refresh All row says `Waiting`), and **the page's tick starts the refresh and opens its dialog when the thread has stopped**; when the dialog closes `pn_dl_start` puts the queue back on a thread and every episode resumes from its `.part`. A refresh that is running when a download is tapped: the download is queued and starts after it (the dialog is modal anyway). If the page is not in front, not on the
  Shows level or the screen is off when the downloads have paused, the refresh is dropped (no dialog is opened in the dark) and the downloads go on. A refresh keeps the `.episodes` line of an episode that has a file or a `.part` (even when the feed no longer lists it); a queued episode with neither can lose its line to a refresh and then fails with `Not found`.
* **Keeping the device awake**: stock standby stops the Wi-Fi and the CPU when nothing plays (found by a device probe, since removed). `patch/podcasts.c` `pg_awake_tick` is a **page-scoped 1 s timer** (armed after a queue, when the page opens with a queue, and after a refresh; it ends when nothing is queued; removed in the window's destroy) that, **on the UI thread and only while a download thread runs**, calls stock
  **`reset_poweroptions_timer(1, 1, 0)`** (`0x4d7874`, pinned: `addresses.md`): its three arguments each guard one countdown, `a0` the standby time (`setstandby_time`), `a1` the auto power-off (`setsavepower_time`), `a2` the screen's light (`setlight_time`); `(1, 1, 0)` sets the first two again and leaves the screen's timer alone, so the screen still turns off and
  the device does not stand by or power off while a download runs. It does no widget work, so it ignores `pod_screen_lit` (it runs dark too); the visual updates (rows, Downloads row) are in `pg_tick`, which keeps the screen-off rule. **The limit**: AGENTS.md allows page-scoped timers only, so the device is kept awake while the Podcasts window **exists** (in front or behind another page). When the window is
  destroyed (the hardware Back key) the download goes on while the device is awake, the timer is gone, and after a standby the stalled transfer ends in a time-out and fails or retries; a **tap on the row (or opening the page, which arms the timer for a queue still there) resumes from the `.part`**. A refresh's dialog does not keep the device awake (its own matter, as before).

## The date (Apple Podcasts style)

`pc_when(pub, now, out, size)` (`pcmodel.c`): under 60 s "Just now"; under 1 h "Nm ago"; under 24 h "Nh ago"; under 7 days "Nd ago"; else "Oct 23" (this year) or "Oct 23, 2025". `now` is `time()`;
the calendar is `localtime` (the one the status bar and the Daily Mix use). The absolute form is used when the clock was never set (its year is before 2024) or the date is more than a minute ahead of it
(up to a minute ahead is "Just now"). The texts are keys of `tools/pod_i18n.py` (13 languages): `Just now`, `%dm ago`, `%dh ago`, `%dd ago`, twelve month texts that carry the day (`Oct %d`, in German `%d. Okt.`, in
Japanese `10月%d日`: the order is the language's) and `, %d` for the year (`DATE_KEYS` tells the tables test these keys have one `%d` though they do not start with it). The lines are made when a tile is first painted, so on
opening a show and whenever the page comes back to the front (`pg_on_fg` drops the tiles): no timer. The longest line (a date with a year, "135 min left") is measured in every language in `test/pod/podcasts.py`
`width_tests` and fits the 274 px row text (Ukrainian "min left" is "Ще %d хв" for that); a longer one would end in an ellipsis.

## Tags (Now Playing, the mini bar and the lock screen show the episode)

Stock `get_id3info` (`0x4dfd58`, run by `start_player` and at each track change) fills `playsong_id3info` (`0xa0d88c`: album +0x404, artist +0x504, title +0x684) from the **file's tags** with `toolsGetMusicInfo`
(`0x5ec6a8`, taglib), skipping the tag read only when the path and CUE start match the cached struct; the record's own strings are used only when `mclGetCueFlag` is set (record CUE start or end > 0: not usable, the
player would seek in the file). It then copies the parsed path into the struct (`jalr strcpy` at `0x4dff4c`: the cache key), and builds `song_name` / `song_artist` from the struct: an empty title becomes the file name, an empty
artist "Unknown" (`0x7466e8`). Tag-less episodes therefore showed "Unknown" and the raw tag title. (A timer route that wrote the globals and the labels from `pc_note` was tried and removed: it lagged a tick and left the lock screen alone.)

**The hook** (hook 4 of `docs/modules/payload.md`, after q2-pod's `ID3_PATH` / `ringnav_id3_path`, `README.md`): the `jalr` at `0x4dff4c` becomes `jal pc_id3_path`; the delay slot still loads `a1` = the record's path, `a0` is the struct, `s0` the record.
`pc_id3_path(info, path)` copies the path (bounded at 0x3ff: the CUE start follows) and returns `info`, exactly stock's `strcpy`; then, **only for a path under `/mnt/mmc/Podcasts/`** (any case) **and without the kill switch**
(`/mnt/mmc/m1plus-off`, `/mnt/data/m1plus-off`): if the playing record (`cc_playing`, the same queue row get_id3info read; its path must match) has a title and an artist that is not empty or "Unknown", title / artist / album come from it
(a record queued by the Podcasts page: the episode title from `.episodes`, else the file name; the show title from `.show`, else the folder as artist and album); otherwise the tags stay as stock found them except an empty or "Unknown"
artist, which becomes the show folder, and an empty album, which becomes the artist. Stock's code after the site copies them into `song_name` / `song_artist`, so Now Playing, the mini bar's source (the record) and the lock screen agree.
No file I/O but the kill-switch check, no allocation; a plain path costs the copy and a prefix test (under 1000 instructions in the test).

**Boot path.** Memory Playback can start a track at boot (`start_player` -> `get_id3info`): `tools/reach.py` reports the site reachable from `platform_init` (over-approximate chain `deqVipPackage_init` -> `get_hifi_lyric_address` -> `sub_500cc0` ->
`get_id3info`). The hook is therefore minimal and falls back to stock for every non-podcast path; a podcast at boot does the kill-switch `access` calls, `mclGetPlayPos` and `deque_at`. The boot guard's crash marker is not consulted (as for the other
page hooks); the SD kill switch is.

Pins (`PODCAST_PINS`): the prologue, the words around the site (`lw t9` strcpy, `lw a0` struct, `sw v0,0x400(s1)`, the `jalr`, the delay slot, `lw t9` memset after it), the `toolsGetMusicInfo` call and the two empty-field tests; data `playsong_id3info`, `song_name`,
`song_artist`. A mismatch refuses the build. Tests: `test/pod/podcasts.py` `tag_tests` runs stock's `get_id3info` for real under Unicorn (callees mocked, `toolsGetMusicInfo` returns the tags a test sets) with the stock word at the site (shows "Unknown") and with the payload;
music, tags that are complete, the cache, both kill switches, a long path and a record with another path; `test/demo_diff.py` allows exactly that one word. Not covered: the Pod's Now Playing level (its own text from the record) and stock list rows.

The level is painted by the content view like the others: **the field** are widgets of the content view (`ps_field`: **300 x 36 at (10, 8), the keyboard page's own rectangles** (x, width, the magnifier at 8, the text from 30 for 228 px, the clear disc in the 36 px box at 264: `PQ_*` = `tools/pod_keyboard.py FIELD_*`; the level's field used to be 296 wide at x 12 with the magnifier 4 px further right, so the glyph jumped 4 px when the keyboard page opened over it with its clear button showing; the payload never moves a magnifier in either field), `#1C1C1E`, 10 px corners, the keyboard page's magnifier `qk_mag`, the hint `Search` grey `#8E8E93` while empty, the text 18 px white cut with an ellipsis by `az_fit`, the clear disc `ps_clear` (`qk_clear`) shown with a text: a tap on it empties the field, drops the rows, cancels what runs and opens the keyboard; insensitive, so a touch on it reaches the content view, `PH_FIELD`), then from `PQ_TOP` 52 the **rows** (the Downloads rows' look and `grid_list_paint`: a 52 px thumbnail at x 10, the name 21 px white, the author 18 px grey, a hairline; 64 px a row, at most 25), the title `Search`, the navbar Back and the round Now Playing.
* **The keyboard** is not embedded in the page but follows the keyboard module's convention (the Playlists page's New playlist does the same): a tap on the field (and the level when it is entered with nothing typed) calls `keyboard_open("Search", "Search", text, pg_srch_done, pg_srch_cancel, KB_LONG)`; its Search key closes the keyboard page and calls back. The callbacks run while that page is closing, so they only record (`pg.sdone` with the text, `pg.sback`) and `pg_srch_pending` acts when the Podcasts page is in front (called from the callback if it is, from `pg_on_fg` and from the tick). Cancel with nothing typed goes back to the Shows level; with a text it stays. `KB_LONG` (255 bytes instead of 63) lets a feed address be typed. The pad of the keyboard hides behind the keyboard page, so the results are never covered: the "keyboard hides when results show" of the design is the page change. *Embedding* the pad in the page (as the unmerged `feat/search` branch does with `keyboard_attach`) would need that branch's keyboard changes; when it is merged this level can switch to it, the field already has the same widget names' look. **Submit** sends the query, no search per keystroke.
* **What is typed** (`pn_classify`): text starting with `http://` or `https://` (any case) that is **not** an Apple Podcasts link is a **feed address**: the preview opens directly. A link whose host is `podcasts.apple.com` and that has `/id<digits>` (the digits as text: Apple's ids pass 32 bits, e.g. `id6811931105`) is looked up: `https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<digits>`, the first result's `feedUrl` is the feed, then the preview; a lookup that gives no feed address ends as `Can't subscribe: Not available` (`PN_NOFEED`). A podcasts.apple.com link without an id, and any other address, is treated as a feed (and fails as one: `Not a podcast feed`). Anything else is a **search term**.
* **The search request** (`pn_search`, one worker job, `PN_KIND_SEARCH`): `https://itunes.apple.com/search?media=podcast&entity=podcast&limit=25&country=US&term=<term>`; the term is UTF-8, URL-encoded (`pn_urlenc`: letters, digits and `-_.~` as they are, every other byte `%XX`; a space is `%20`, `&` is `%26`; at most 63 bytes typed unless KB_LONG, the request buffer is 400 bytes: a longer term is `PN_MEM`, nothing starts). **The country is always US**: `PN_COUNTRY` in `patch/pcnet.h`, the one place; a region switch comes later. The same `pn_easy` as a feed's (the firmware CA bundle, verification on and never switched off, 16 redirects limited to http(s), fail on HTTP error, time-outs, `M1PlusMusic/1.0`, every encoding). The answer is collected in a 96 KB buffer (`PN_JSON_CAP` 98304; a device probe saw 44 KB for 25 results): an answer that goes on is cut there (the write callback stops the transfer) and the results that were whole are used. Guards first (`pn_guard`: card, Wi-Fi, clock, CA bundle: `No Wi-Fi`, `Set the date first`, `Certificates missing`, `Storage unavailable`). Failures are the existing words: no connection / time-out `Can't connect`, certificate `Certificate problem`, HTTP 403 / 429 (the store's rate limit, about 20 calls a minute) / 404 / an HTML page / JSON without a results array `Server error`, no memory `Not enough memory`. **No result** is OK with 0 results: `No results`.
* **One worker, never beside a refresh or a download (the choice: the downloads pause for it, as they do for a refresh)**: `pg_srch_pump` (the submit and the 1 s tick) asks a running download thread to pause (`pn_dl_pause`: the transfer is aborted at once, the queue is kept), waits for it, and starts the job when no thread runs; a refresh in progress is waited for (never cancelled); a search or preview that runs is **cancelled** by a newer job (`pn_cancel`: the transfer-info callback aborts libcurl's transfer) and the new one starts at the next tick. `pn_busy` stays a refresh's (`pn_job_busy` is a search's or a preview's); `pn_refresh`, `pn_search` and `pn_preview` refuse with `PN_BUSY` while any worker runs, `pn_dl_start` starts nothing. When the job is over the tick calls `pn_dl_start` (and the downloads' own awake timer does, as after a refresh): the downloads go on. Leaving the level (Back), the preview taking the worker and the window being destroyed (the hardware Back key) cancel the job; destroy also removes the thumbnails and drops a held preview.
* **The extractor** (`patch/pcjson.c`, `pj_results(json, n, out, max)`): demo has no JSON library and the answer is untrusted, so it is a walk, not a parser: iterative (no recursion, no allocation), bounded by `n` on every read and by each field's size on every write, depth at most `PJ_DEPTH` 32 (deeper ends the walk, keeping what was found). It finds the top-level `"results"` array and, in each object, takes **only** `collectionId` (a number: more than 32 bits is `0xffffffff`, a negative one 0; the id is not used for anything), `collectionName` (80 bytes), `artistName` (56), `feedUrl` (`PC_URL` 320) and `artworkUrl100` (160); every other member, nested value, type mismatch and duplicate (the last wins) is skipped by its brackets and quotes. Strings are decoded to UTF-8: `\" \\ \/`, the control escapes (a space), `\uXXXX` (a surrogate pair is one 4 byte character; a lone surrogate, `\u0000` and a `\u` without four hex digits are dropped), a raw control byte is a space, raw bytes that are not valid UTF-8 (a lone lead, an overlong, a surrogate, past U+10FFFF) are dropped; a string that does not fit is cut **on a character boundary** (a prefix: the first character that does not fit ends it), and an **address that does not fit is none** (`""`: a cut address is no address; the result stays and is `Not available`). An object is a result when it has a name or a feed; one cut short by the end of the text is not kept (the 96 KB cap in the middle of a result). Returns the count, or -1 when there is no results array. Tests (`test/pod/pcjson.py`, 66 assertions): three real answers (All In, 故事FM, Top of Japan: `test/data/search/*.json`, the store's own, against `json.loads`), every escape and surrogate case, the cuts at 79 / 55 bytes for 1 to 4 byte characters, missing fields, ids, nesting at 32 / 33 and 5000 unclosed objects, truncation at every 7th byte (exactly the results complete before the cut), the 96 KB cut, and **fuzzing** (260 random cuts, byte flips and junk texts: the input ends at an unmapped page and so does the output array, so any overrun faults; every string terminated inside its field, valid UTF-8, no control byte, the count within max).
* **The results** (`pn.sres[25]`, a record in `.scratch` the worker fills and the page only reads once `pn_search_state` has left `PN_S_QUERY`; 15 KB): rows of the Downloads level's geometry (`tools/pod_show.py search_layout`; the tile ring `pg.g` is reset for each answer): the show's name and its author in grey; **a result without `feedUrl` says `Not available` in its grey line, is dimmed (`PQ_DIM`, an ARGB black over the row) and takes no tap** (`pg_hit` returns none for it). The states: `Searching…` (from the submit until the answer: also while the job waits for the downloads), `No results`, the failure's words, `Loading…` while a preview is fetched (the rows are hidden meanwhile). They are the `label_status` the empty states use, under the field. A new search clears the rows at once.
* **The thumbnails**: after the answer the same worker fetches each result's `artworkUrl100` **one by one, in order** (about 5 KB, a baseline JPEG; at most `PN_THUMB_CAP` 256 KB, the same `pn_easy`) into `/mnt/mmc/.m1plus/podcasts/search/t<n>.jpg` (`.png` for a PNG: `pc_pic_info` says; `pc_cover_ok` refuses a picture the stock decoder would choke on): a download file `t<n>.src` that is renamed when it is a picture and removed when it is not. The page's loader (`pg_on_load`, one a 16 ms tick, only while the screen is lit) decodes the ones that came (`pn_search_thumb`) through the Albums grid's cover path (`grid_art_url`, as every cover of the page), the rows in view first; the placeholder disc shows until then (and for ever for a result whose picture failed or has no address). The page notices an arrival from the worker's generation (`pn_search_gen`) in its tick. **Fetching stops when the user leaves** (Back, a preview, destroy: `pn_cancel`; the transfer in progress is aborted and the loop ends); thumbnails not fetched are not resumed when the level is entered again (the answer and the pictures that came stay, the rest keep the disc). The files are the cache: a new search removes the old ones (`pn_search_clean`), destroying the window removes them, a card with leftovers (a power cut) is cleaned by the next search.
* **The preview** (`pn_preview`, `PN_KIND_PREVIEW`): a tap on a result (or a feed address or an Apple link typed) fetches and parses the feed **as a refresh does** (`pn_fetch`: the push parser `pf_*`, 8 MiB cap, kept when a feed and an item were seen), after the lookup when there is an id, and shrinks the feed's picture to `search/preview.jpg` (`pv_shrink` / `pv_shrink_png`: the existing cover path, 192 px). **Nothing is a show folder yet**: the parsed feed is **held in memory** (`pn.prev`, a `pf_parser_t`, about 25 KB: the title, the picture address, the author, the category, the summary and the 20 newest items), handed to the page when the worker has ended (a cancelled or failed job frees it itself; the page frees it with `pn_preview_drop` on Back, on Subscribe and on destroy), plus the cover file. The page makes a `pc_show_t` of it in the spare slot `shows[PC_MAX_SHOWS]` (folder name `.pv<hash>`, so the tone cache's key is its own; the cover is the preview file: `pg_cover_url`) and `pc_ep_t`s (remote, no file) from the items, and opens the **Show page in a preview state** (`pg.preview`): the cover tinted as the page is, the title, the description, "Category · Author", **the Latest Episode pill is `Subscribe`** (white with the dark label, no glyph, at least `PQ_SUB_MIN` 150 px wide, the label centred), the episodes as **normal cells without the pill row** (the date line, the title in the normal colour, two lines, the description, two lines; no pill, no "...", no badge, nothing reserved for them: a dedicated cell height `PS_PCELL_H` 137 in `patch/offsets.inc`, `pg_cell_h()`, instead of `PS_CELL_H` 173), a tap on a cell does nothing, the navbar has no "..." (Now Playing is the round disc), **no sheet** (`pg_sheet_open` refuses), no select mode, no refresh reading of the model (`pg_reload_eps` and the tick's reload skip the preview). Back drops the held feed and returns to the search level with its rows. **A failed first fetch** (403, 404, not a feed, a certificate, no connection, a lookup without a feed) holds nothing, makes no folder and no file under `Podcasts`, and says `Can't subscribe: <the reason>` as a toast (`Server error`, `Feed not found`, `Not a podcast feed`, ..., `Not available` for the lookup); the page stays on the search level. Where the toast is wider than 300 px (18 px text) in a language, only the reason is shown, and where even that is too wide only `Can't subscribe` (`pg_srch_fail`; checked in 13 languages).
* **Subscribe** (`pn_subscribe`, on the UI thread, no network: an import writes on the UI thread too): makes **exactly what an import and its first refresh make**: the folder named from the feed's title (`pf_folder_name`; when that is empty or taken by someone else, the import's name, `<host>-<hash>`), `.episodes` (`pf_render_episodes` with no old file) and `.show` (`pf_render_show`: the feed address, the title, the picture address, `checked` now, the author, the first category, the summary), both atomically (temp file, fsync, rename), and `cover.jpg` (the preview's picture renamed into the folder; with none the next refresh fetches it), the held feed dropped. A write that fails removes every file and the folder it made (`PN_STORAGE`, `Can't subscribe: Storage unavailable`, the preview stays); 40 shows already is the same. The page then reads the folders again and **becomes the real Show page of the new show in place** (`pg_open_show` on the same level, at the top: the preview's shorter cells do not line up with the real ones): the normal cells with their download pills ("↓ 48 MB"), badges, the "..." and the sheets, exactly as when opened from the grid; its **Back returns to the search results** (`pg.from_search`), the next one to the Shows level. **Already subscribed** (the same feed address, any case): the real Show page of that show opens at once (no fetch, no preview: checked when a result is tapped, when an address is typed, and after the lookup of an Apple link), the header pill is the normal Latest Episode pill, Back returns to the results. There is no "Subscribed" state any more (the key was removed). Test: `test/pod/pcsearch.py` `subscribe_tests` compares an import plus a refresh of the same feed (the same `.episodes` and `.show`, byte for byte, the same 192 px `cover.jpg`).
* **Limits**: 25 results, 96 KB of answer, 256 KB a thumbnail, 255 typed bytes (a typed feed address always fits `PC_URL` 320; a result's feed address over 319 bytes is none, so `Not available`), one worker. The country is US only. Search words and the addresses typed are sent to Apple's servers and to the feed's host: the Settings note says so. The scratch directory `/mnt/mmc/.m1plus/podcasts/search/` is the only file area (no write when the card is not mounted: the guard). The rate limit is respected by the design (a search per submit, one thumbnail request after another, nothing per keystroke) and not otherwise enforced: ten quick submits of different terms are ten requests.
* **Screen off**: no visual work while the screen is off. A job already started goes on (the worker never looks at the screen); the page's tick does nothing for the level in the dark (no rows, no status line, no preview opened, no thumbnail decoded: `pg_srch_sync` runs only lit; the loader returns at once); at the first lit tick the answer, the thumbnails and the preview that came meanwhile are taken in. A job that waits for the downloads to pause starts from the lit tick (a submit starts one at once when nothing runs, light or no).
* **Device checks (not run)**: Settings > Library > Podcast Search on: the Podcasts page shows the Search capsule (both shapes: with and without `subscriptions.txt`) and the glass pieces join (the middle piece's ends, the pressed highlight on the dots); a tap opens the level and our keyboard (long text, the Chinese layer); Search with a term shows `Searching…` and then up to 25 rows within a second or two on Wi-Fi, thumbnails fill in one at a time while scrolling is smooth (decoding is one per 16 ms tick); a term with CJK ("日谈公园"); a result with no feed is dimmed; a tap opens the preview (`Loading…`, then the header with the real cover and the tint, `Subscribe` white, the episodes greyed); Subscribe makes the folder (`ls` the card: `.episodes`, `.show`, `cover.jpg`, no `search` leftovers other than the cache directory) and the Show page works as an imported one; Subscribe on a feed already there; a feed address pasted (63+ bytes) and an Apple link (`https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-daily/id1200361736`, and an id past 32 bits); Search while a download runs (it pauses, the search runs, the download resumes with Range); a search while Wi-Fi is off (`No Wi-Fi`), with a wrong date (`Set the date first`); the store's rate limit (a burst of submits: `Server error` once the limit bites); the screen off during the search and the thumbnails; Back mid-thumbnails and straight into a refresh (a second or less of `PN_BUSY` is silent: the refresh row ignores a tap that finds the worker still ending); the hardware Back key out of the page with a search running (no crash, the cache directory empty at the next open); turn the switch off in Settings and return: the button is gone and the Shows page is as before.
