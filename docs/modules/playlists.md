# Playlists (grid of mosaics)

Not in the old section numbering (a feature added after V2.1oA). Code: `patch/playlists.c`, `patch/grid.c` (`grid_mosaic`), the page `tools/pod_ui.py` (`build_playlists_page`), the row `tools/pod_pages.py` (`PLAYLISTS`, `ACT_PLAYLISTS`), the preview `tools/preview_playlists.py`, the test `test/pod/playlists.py`. **No hook.** The menu's Playlists row (`ACT_PLAYLISTS`, formerly Home id 14, the stock `playlist_page`) opens `ui/m1plus_playlists.bin`; a window that will not open falls back to the stock page through the Home dispatcher (`pod_home_open(14)`), which also stays reachable from Shanling Home.

## What the user sees

- **Two modes of one page, as Albums** (`docs/modules/albums.md`): the navbar reads Back, "Playlists", the **"..." button** at x 168 (46 x 50, where Albums has its Cover Flow button; the title ends there), the **view toggle** `btn_albumview` at x 214 (the Albums button: the glyph of the view a tap switches to, `pod_nav_list` / `pod_nav_grid`), Now Playing. The toggle switches the mode **in place** (`pl_set_mode`: tiles dropped and remade in the new size and shape, the content resized, the first playlist in view kept, a coasting list stopped), and saves it in **`/mnt/data/m1plus-playlists`** (`1` grid, `0` list, anything else the grid; its own file, apart from Albums' `m1plus-albums`), so it survives re-open.
- **List mode**: 64 px rows (`LIST_ROW_H`), the 52 px mosaic (quadrants of 26 px; one album full size; none the placeholder; `LIST_R` corners cut in the alpha) at (10, 4), the name in 21 px white and "N tracks" in 18 px `#AAAAAA` from x 74 (the styles of the hidden labels `label_row_title` / `label_row_sub`), a `#222222` hairline, the pressed row `#333333`. The row painter is shared with Albums: **`grid_list_paint`** in `patch/grid.c` (moved there from `patch/albums.c`, which calls it). A tap on a row (any x) opens the playlist.
- Grid mode: the Albums grid's page (navbar as above), under it a stock `list_view` > `scroll_view` + `scroll_bar_m` holding one content view, two columns of 144 px tiles with the shelf's 8 px corners. Under a tile the playlist's name (18 px white) and "N tracks" (15 px `#AAAAAA`, the album page's `%d track` / `%d tracks` keys). A tap on a cell opens the playlist.
- **The tile** is a 2 x 2 mosaic of the playlist's albums: four albums fill the quadrants (A B / C D); three repeat the first (A B / C A); two (A B / B A); one is that cover full size; no album with a cover (an empty playlist, or none of its albums has a cache file) is the stock placeholder disc.
- **The "..." menu** is a popup card under the button in the glass of Now Playing's More sheet (`docs/modules/playmore.md` "Glass"), four 50 px rows filling it edge to edge (the sheet's rows; no padding, so the last row is as tall as the others), each a 20 px glyph at x 16 (2 px stroke; the sheet's 24 px looked big beside the 18 px labels on the device) (New `list-plus`, Import `file-input`, Export `file-output`, Delete `trash`: `mod_icons.PL_MENU_ICONS`, images `pl_ic_*`) and its label from x 48, over a full-window scrim that dims the page as the sheet does (`#00000082`, the sheet's `alpha=130`; a tap outside closes it). Rows: **New playlist**, **Import playlist**, **Export playlist**, **Delete Playlists** (it starts select mode on this page, below); transparent, pressed 6 % white (`#FFFFFF10`), a 13 % white hairline (`#FFFFFF22`) above every row but the first, as the sheet's rows.
- **The card fits the language.** It is as wide as the widest of its four labels plus the glyph's 48 px before it and 16 px after, at least 180 px, at most 292 (the widest language, Spanish "Importar listas de reproducción" 227 px at 18, fits), its right edge at 314 under the button: English, Chinese and Italian 180, Thai 184, Korean 196, Ukrainian 219, French 234, Polish 261, Russian 269, German 279, Japanese 288, Spanish 291.
- **Select mode (the menu's Delete Playlists), in the Songs page's manner** (`docs/modules/songs.md` "Select"). The marks (`pod_make_mark`, `patch/common.c`) are cut to the ring's circle (radius 11 in their 26 px square, coverage alpha): the decode flattens them over black, which showed as a black square on every cover (device test of oWA). The row keeps its name and its trash glyph: select mode here exists for deleting (it is the stock Playlists page's multi-select, whose only actions are export and delete; export stays the menu's row), so a separate "Select" row would only lead to Delete; the tap on the row is the Select button of the Songs page. In select mode the navbar's Back becomes the glass x (`ah_nav_close`), the title reads **N Selected** (cut at x 208), the text button **Select All** (all, or none when all are marked) is at the right, and "...", the toggle and Now Playing go (hidden and insensitive). Every cell has a 26 px mark (the stock `unselect` / `selected` images) at the bottom right of its cover, 6 px in; in list mode a mark column of 30 px (`SG_SEL_COL`) opens left of each row and the cover and the texts move right by it (`grid_list_paint`'s new `shift` argument; the row's texts are made again at the narrower width, `pl_retext`). A tap marks and unmarks (it opens nothing; a drag marks nothing); no pressed fill. A 64 px bar (the Songs page's: black, a #222222 hairline) holds one flat **Delete** pill across it (300 x 40 at x 10, the glyph `sg_trash`, `pod_centre_pill`), the content is 64 px longer so the last row scrolls clear. Back (the x) leaves the mode first; a page read again (the window comes back to the front, after a delete) is out of it, since the marks were of the old list. **Delete** is the stock handler's sequence (`pl_on_delete`; evidence below): nothing marked is the stock message `msg_delplaylistfailed`; the stock confirm dialog with `msg_delplaylist` (Yes, 0xa, anything else ends it, the mode stays); then the stock list is read again and must be what the page shows (as many rows with the same ids in the same order, else nothing is deleted and the page reads again), the stock vector is filled from the marks (`select_recordinit(n)`, `set_selectindex(i)`), `batch_delete_file(0xf00b, *pdeq_playlist, the vector, 0, 0)`, the message `msg_delplaylistsuccess`, select mode ends and the page reads again. Where the page cannot do it (the asset lacks `btn_selall`, `pl_bar` or `pl_del`, the marks do not decode, no selection bitset or vector) the row opens the stock Playlists page as before (`pod_home_open(14)`, its multi-select deletes); with no playlist the row only closes the menu.
- Empty (no playlist): the grid is hidden and a centred "No playlists" shows; the menu is still there.

## The stock database calls (reverse engineering, V2.1 `demo`)

- Playlists are rows of the `playlist` table of `/mnt/data/database.db` (`toolsInitDatabase` 0x5ed734; `create table %s(id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY, filename VARCHAR(512), f...` 0x757948 / 0x757aec). A row with `id <= 100` is a playlist (`getAllPlayList` 0x4ec4e8, `select * from playlist where id <= 100`: name in `REC_TITLE`, id in `REC_ID` and `REC_DISC`, which `createPlayListItem` 0x4eeca8 stores alike, type 4), a row with `id > 100` is a song linked by `cd_num` = the playlist id (`getMusicByPlayList` 0x4eddac, `select * from playlist where id > 100 and cd_num=%d`, no ORDER BY: insertion order).
- **Open question 1, the song columns.** The row callback (0x4e9058) takes **18 columns**, the same as a library song (`stSongInfo`, 0x58 bytes): a playlist song carries `REC_ALBUM` and `REC_ARTIST` as they were when it was added, so the Cover Flow cache key (`cc_album_key`: FNV-1a of artist then album) is computed from the row itself. That is the key of the album record only when the track's artist is the one SQLite picked for the album (`getAllAlbum` is `select id,album,songer,fileurl ... group by album COLLATE NOCASE`: one arbitrary artist per album), so a track of a compilation can miss: the page then takes the key of the library album of that name (`cc_nocase` on `REC_ALBUM` in `cc_albums()`, the Albums page's copy, loaded at the first scan), tested.
- `loadLocalClassList(0xf00b)` (0x4ee504; `getAllPlayList` at 0x4ee618, assigned to `*pdeq_playlist` 0xa045d0 at 0x4ee63c) lists the playlists; with `classinfo[1]` (0xa0c768 + 4) = a playlist id, `loadLocalClassList(0xff13)` (0x4ee744: `getMusicByPlayList(classinfo[1])`, assigned to `*pdeq_showlist`) lists its songs. Both return the deque size.
- **Open question 2, opening a playlist.** The stock Playlists page (`playlist_page_init` 0x53eb24: the page context into `0xa00000 + 0x6f50 / 0x6f54`, `loadLocalClassList(0xf00b)`, the rows built by 0x53e74c: `playlist_firstitem_layout` 0x53e064 = the create row, `playlist_seconditem_layout` 0x53e19c = Import / Export, `playlist_listitem_layout` 0x53e44c = one per playlist, each registered with the shared row handler 0x53d300) opens a playlist in **mode 0** (`0xa00000 + 0x6f50` <= 0, the page opened from Home with context 0): `snprintf(nagvation_title 0xa0533c, 0x200, "%s", name)` (0x53d5b8), `classinfo[1] = REC_DISC` of the row (0x53d5f4), **`navigator_to_with_context("allmusic_page", 0xff13)`** (0x53d5e8 .. 0x53d428; the context is the class, a value). `allmusic_page_init` 0x528968 then runs `loadLocalClassList(0xff13)` itself and copies `nagvation_title` into the page's title. `pl_open` does exactly those three steps, so play, delete and reorder are the stock page's.
- **Open question 3, create / import / export.**
  - *Create*: the handler at 0x53e8e4 calls `navigator_to_with_context("dialog/kbaddplaylist_dialog", buf)` (modal; the dialog stores the context pointer at `0xa00000 + 0x39e0`, `dialog_kbaddplaylist_dialog_init` 0x477c24, the edit's max is 127 characters; returns 0 for accepted), then `createPlayListItem(*pdeq_playlist, buf)` (-1 full: the message `msg_playlistfull`, -2 exists: `msg_playlistexist`, else the new id, in `addPlayList` 0x4ee1cc -> `toolsInsertDbTable`), then rebuilds its own list (0x53e74c, which looks up the stock page's widgets: not callable from our page). `pl_create` repeats the first three steps with a zeroed 256-byte buffer and the stock list read first (`createPlayListItem` reads it for the free id and the duplicate name check), shows the two messages in the stock message box, and the page reads again.
  - *Import / Export*: the click handlers of the stock rows (static, 0x53dedc and 0x53dd34, `ret_t (ctx, event)`, neither reads its arguments) ask with `dialog/confirminfo_dialog` (`msg_importplaylist` / `msg_exportplaylist`), run `importPlayList` 0x4ef394 / `exportPlayList` 0x4ef0b0(`all`, `*pdeq_playlist`, `*0xa045e0`) over `/mnt/mmc/_explaylist_data` and report in the stock message box. They are **called as they are** (pinned by prologue and call words, `tools/pod.py` `PLAYLIST_PINS`); export reads `navigation_status` (0xa05a24, 1 = the lists' multi-select mode) to choose all or the selected playlists, so the page stores 0 there first (the stock pages reset it at their init).
  - *Delete* (the evidence for the page's select mode). The stock handler `0x53dbcc` (registered on the multi-select mode of the stock Playlists page: `navbar_btnmulselect_style`, then select-all, `navbar_btndel_style`, `navbar_btncancel_style`, the navbar built by `0x53d5f8`) touches **no widget** and does not rebuild its page; its whole sequence, read from the disassembly: (1) `select_recordempty()` `0x4da60c`; if nothing is selected a `dialog/msginfo_dialog` context (kind 1, 2000 ms, `getFormatString("msg_delplaylistfailed")` at +8) and it ends; (2) else a `dialog/confirminfo_dialog` context (kind **1**, the text `msg_delplaylist`; the Songs delete uses kind 3, which has the checkbox) and the call returns 0xa for Yes (`0x53dcc0`, `0x53dcc4`: anything else ends it); (3) **`batch_delete_file(*0x97cad4, *pdeq_playlist, *0xa045e0, 0, 0)`** (`0x53dcd0` .. `0x53dcf0`: the browsing class `g_class_type` is 0xf00b on that page; the fifth argument, `delete_file`, is 0: no file belongs to a playlist); (4) the msginfo message `msg_delplaylistsuccess`. `batch_delete_file` takes the class from its argument: its jump table `0x74b7b0` (`word + gp 0xa03ed0`, one word for each class `0xf003 .. 0xf00b`) sends 0xf00b (the ninth word, `0x74b7d0` = `0xffade414`, 0x4e22e4) to the loop whose body is `0x4e2264`: for each `i` below `vector_size(selection)` and `deque_size(list)` with `vector_at(selection, i)` non-zero, **`deletePlaylist(*deque_at(list, i))` `0x4edf98` and `deleteAllMusicByPlaylist(*deque_at(list, i))` `0x4edfdc`** (the record's first word is the id: `REC_ID`, as `REC_DISC`), then the common tail (`*0xa05a5a = 1`, the \"library changed\" flag). The class is passed by the page (0xf00b), not read from `g_class_type`, so the page does not depend on what the browsing class was. The selection vector is the one the Songs page fills (`select_recordinit`, `set_selectindex`, `docs/modules/songs.md`); pinned (`tools/pod.py` `PLAYLIST_PINS`): the handler's Yes test and its `batch_delete_file` call words, the class's jump-table word and the two `bal` words of the loop. **Chosen path: select mode on our page** (above), the stock page only as the fallback.

## The page (`patch/playlists.c`)

- Widgets (`tools/pod_ui.py`): `playlists_listview` > `playlists_scrollview` > `view_grid` + `scroll_bar_m` (the Albums structure: the mobile bar is required, `docs/modules/albums.md`), the hidden `label_status`, `label_grid_title` / `label_grid_sub` (the text styles), then **last** (above everything) the hidden, insensitive `pl_scrim` (a transparent button, 320 x 450) and `pl_card` (a `view`, `round_radius` 14) with the row buttons `pl_item0..3` (each holds an insensitive `hscroll_label`). No widget is renamed or removed; hide = `visible=false` + `sensitive=false`, as everywhere.
- **Data.** `pl_load` (at open and whenever the window gets `EVT_WINDOW_TO_FG`: back from the songs page, the stock Playlists page or a dialog) runs the stock query `loadLocalClassList(0xf00b)`, copies the rows (`cc_copy_list`), drops every tile and scan, and sizes the content. Nothing survives a visit, so a playlist edited in the stock songs page needs no invalidation. At most `PL_MAX` (100: the id range) playlists. Per playlist the loader's **scan** (`pl_scan`, one playlist a tick) sets `classinfo[1]`, runs `loadLocalClassList(0xff13)`, takes the count, and from the first `PL_SCAN` (50) songs the first four **distinct** albums that have a cache file (`cc_url`; a key is looked at once; the library lookup above when the song's own key has no file; this choice is `grid_mosaic_keys` in `patch/grid.c`, which the Songs page's playlist header calls too, `docs/modules/songs.md` "A playlist's header"). The list of every song is the stock one (`*pdeq_showlist`, replaced by the next query), so the page holds only four keys per playlist (`info_t`, 24 bytes).
- **Tiles and the loader.** The Albums page's ring (`GRID_TILES` 12 tiles of 144 x 144 x 4 = 81 KB, plus the placeholder; the same 1.1 MB bound as Albums, plus one 72 x 72 quadrant buffer of 20 KB and the library copy for the lookup, which Albums holds too). `grid_mosaic` (`patch/grid.c`): one source per call, `cc_decode` box-reduced to 72 px (the area average of `cc_box`) into the quadrant buffer and copied into every quadrant that shows it; the bitmap exists, grey and unfinished, until the last source, which rounds the corners (`GRID_R`, blended into black once: the tile is opaque) and sets `art` 1, so paint shows the placeholder until then. One album is `grid_art` (the cover at 144). The page timer (`pl_on_load`, 16 ms) does ONE step a tick (a scan or one source) for the cell nearest the middle of the view, the cells in view before the row ahead; it does nothing while the `scroll_view` drags or flings (`pl_moving`, the Albums page's check of the stock fields), and is re-armed by `EVT_SCROLL` / `EVT_SCROLL_END`. **It stops when the window goes behind another** (`EVT_WINDOW_TO_BG`: the timer removed, `pl.bg` keeps a late scroll event from re-arming it) and restarts with the to-foreground's read: a scan sets `classinfo[1]` and replaces `*pdeq_showlist`, which the stock songs page of the playlist just tapped draws its rows from and plays (a tap before the mosaics in view were done showed and played another playlist's songs). Paint never decodes and never queries. The tile's texts are made when the tile is, with the count blank until the playlist is scanned and re-made after the scan.
- **Touch.** As the Albums grid: DOWN remembers the cell, ABORT (the scroll_view took a drag) forgets it, UP within `SC_DRAG_PX` of the press on the same cell opens the playlist (`pl_open`) **in our Songs page** (`songs_open_playlist`: the name as the title, the id in `classinfo[1]`; docs/modules/songs.md "The other lists"); only when that page will not open does it take the stock way of open question 2 (the stock songs page).
- **Menu.** `btn_more` toggles card and scrim (`pl_menu`), the scrim closes it. At every open `pl_fit` measures the four labels (`widget_measure_text`: the label's own font and its translated text) and sets the card's x and width (`PL_CARD_RIGHT` - w, `PL_CARD_MIN`..`PL_CARD_MAX`, `patch/offsets.inc`), the rows' and labels' widths; a label missing or not measurable leaves the asset's 292 px card. A row closes the menu first, then: New -> `pl_create`; Import / Export -> `stock_playlist_import` / `_export` with `navigation_status` = 0; Delete -> select mode (`pl_select(1)`; `pod_home_open(14)` where the page cannot). After New, Import and Export the page runs `pl_load`.
- **The glass.** `pl_glass.png` (`assets/icons/png/`, installed with the `np_*` images) = `mod_icons.glass_image(72, 200, 14, fill=GLASS_SHEET_FILL)`: the sheet's pane (fill alpha 0.88, rim, inner line) with all four corners rounded, as tall as the card. The card draws it as its `bg_image` with `bg_image_draw_type` `patch3_x` (no fill, no `round_radius`): stock normalises the inline name (`style_normalize_value` 0x65a1f4, `strstr "image_draw_type"` -> `image_draw_type_find`; `patch3_x` = 13 in the table at 0x9050d8), `canvas_draw_image_ex` case 13 calls `canvas_draw_image_patch3_x` 0x647a08, which draws `min(dst.w, img.w) / 3` = 24 px of each end as they are and stretches the middle third. The gradient is vertical only and the middle third is the same in every column (test), so any width from 72 up is exact. First user of a stretched draw type in this app (stock assets use none); the test checks the stock words it relies on.
- Texts: the stock keys `playlist_create`, `playlist_import`, `playlist_export` (their stock translations in the 13 languages), `msg_playlistfull`, `msg_playlistexist`; stock `msg_delplaylist`, `msg_delplaylistfailed`, `msg_delplaylistsuccess` (the select mode's confirm dialog and messages) and the Songs page's `%d Selected`, `Select All`, `Delete`; ours `Delete Playlists`, `No playlists` (`tools/pod_i18n.py`), `%d track(s)`.
- Pins (`tools/pod.py`): `createPlayListItem` 0x4eeca8 (a named function), the data words `pdeq_playlist` 0xa045d0, `nagvation_title` 0xa0533c, `navigation_status` 0xa05a24, and `PLAYLIST_PINS` for the two static click handlers. The payload grew to 103,304 bytes.

## Not verified on the device

Everything above is checked on the emulated payload and the real stock code where the harness runs it; not seen on the device: the popup's glass card (`patch3_x` stretching `pl_glass` to the fitted width, the scrim's dim, the hairlines) and its row hit-testing, the keyboard dialog opening from a click handler of our page (the stock page does the same from its own), the stock import / export dialogs over our page, the "..." glyph's look, the time of a scan on a large playlist table (one `select ... where id > 100 and cd_num=` per playlist, one a 16 ms tick), the select mode (its look: the marks at the covers' corner, the pill across the bar; the stock confirm dialog kind 1 and `batch_delete_file(0xf00b, ...)` called from our page: read from the disassembly and run in the emulation with the call mocked, never run for real) and the stock Playlists page as its fallback.
